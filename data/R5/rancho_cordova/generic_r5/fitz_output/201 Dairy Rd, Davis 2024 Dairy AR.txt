RE:  2024 Annual Report for the UC Davis Animal Science Dairy Facilities 
 
 
Enclosed are the completed documents for the submission of the Annual Report for the UC 
Davis Animal Science Dairy at the University of California, Davis, for January 1, 2024 through 
December 31, 2024 (Attachment A). This submission fulfills the annual reporting requirement 
under the Waste Discharge Requirements General Order for Existing Milk Cow Dairies (Order 
No. R5-2013-0122). As of December 21, 2018, the Central Valley Water Board approved the 
merger of the UC Davis Animal Science Beef Feedlot and Beef Teaching Facility with the UC 
Davis Dairy under the Reissued General Order. Based on this merger, the 2024 Annual Report 
covers the Dairy, Beef Feedlot, and Beef Teaching Facilities.   
 
This submittal includes the following attachments: 
 
Attachment A – Annual Dairy Report for 2024 
Attachment B – Analytical Laboratory Reports  
Attachment C – Manure Manifest  
 
The following sections describe the Nutrient Management Plan, Supply Water, Waste 
Management, and billing information for the UC Davis Animal Science Dairy Facilities. No 
parcels have been used for land application for at least 5 years.  
 
Nutrient Management Plan 
 
The Nutrient Management Plan (Plan) was last updated in 2015.  The Plan was prepared and 
approved by a certified nutrient management specialist, and a copy is maintained on-site.  The 
Plan was not updated with the recent facility merger discussed above, as the facilities have no 
active land application activities, nor any plans for the future.

Supply Water 
Supply water for the production facility is supplied by the campus's domestic water system. It 
comes from six on-campus wells and surface water from the Sacramento River. UC Davis 
tests the campus domestic water supply (Water System No. 5710009) as required by the 
United States Environmental Protection Agency and the State Water Resources Control Board 
Division of Drinking Water. The analytical data for the supply water is included in Attachment 
B. The supply water used at the Dairy Facilities is solely for animal consumption. It was not
used for irrigation on nearby properties (G-tract). No irrigation water was used for the G-tract
parcel planted with wheat hay, and therefore, no sample was collected for nitrate-nitrite in
2024.
Waste Management 
Liquid waste and stormwater runoff from the Animal Science Dairy production facility and the 
Beef Feedlot discharge to the sanitary sewer, which is treated by the campus National 
Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permitted Wastewater Treatment Plant 
(Permit # CA0077895).  Updated Waste Management Plans were submitted to the Central 
Valley Regional Water Quality Board in March 2019 for the Beef Feedlot and the Beef 
Teaching Facility. Manure was not applied to land during 2024 but was manifested off-site to a 
farm. Therefore, no stormwater discharge samples were collected. The stockpiled manure was 
sampled twice in 2024 and is presented in Attachment B.

ATTACHMENTS
The following lists the required documents that should be attached to the Annual Report when submitted .
Annual Dairy Facility Assessment
Provide an Annual Dairy Facility Assessment (an update to the Preliminary Dairy Facility Assessment in Attachment A) for each reporting period.  On the PDFA Final 
page, click on the ADFA Report button to generate an ADFA report after updating information as needed .
Manure/Process Wastewater Tracking Manifests
Provide copies of all manure/process wastewater tracking manifests for the reporting period, signed by both the owner/operator and the hauler.
Corrective Actions Documents
Provide records documenting any corrective actions taken to correct deficiencies noted as a result of the inspections required in the Monitoring Requirements of the 
General Order. Deficiencies not corrected in 30 days must be accompanied by an explanation of the factors preventing immediate correction.
Groundwater Monitoring
Dischargers that monitor supply wells or subsurface (tile) drainage systems, or that have monitoring well systems must submit monitoring results as directed in the 
General Order, Groundwater Reporting Section starting on page MRP-13.
Storm Water Monitoring
Dischargers that are required to monitor storm water more frequently than required in the General Order must submit monitoring results as directed in the General Order, 
Storm Water Reporting Section on page MRP-14.
