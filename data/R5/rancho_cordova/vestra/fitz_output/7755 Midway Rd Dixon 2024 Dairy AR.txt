ANNUAL REPORTING NOTES AND LIMITATIONS 
 
Field 9 was not planted with the corn crop during the 2024 reporting year. No freshwater or process 
wastewater was applied to the forage crop on Fields 2 and 3 during the reporting year. Irrigation wells 
IR-6, IR-7, IR-8, IR-9 and IR-10 were not operating when irrigation well samples were collected during 
the 2024 reporting year. Analytical data from samples collected from these wells during previous 
reporting years (2021 and 2022) were used for freshwater applications from these wells. Fresh water 
samples are not analyzed for total nitrogen; therefore, the nitrate-nitrogen concentration for each source 
was entered as the total nitrogen concentration for land applications of fresh water.  
 
For each field, the volume of fresh water and process water was totaled for each quarter and entered as 
a single land application event for each quarter. Dates for each individual land application event are 
included in Exhibit 3. Due to the amount of process wastewater and fresh water that runs off the fields 
to the tailwater return system during irrigation events, application volumes were decreased by one-third 
of what was recorded in the land application records when this runoff was observed. All manure 
exports were entered as a single event since the operator, hauling entity, and recipient were the same for 
each export.
