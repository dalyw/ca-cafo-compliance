PRIORITY REPORTING OF SIGNIFICANT EVENTS 
 
The General Order requires a Discharger to report any non-compliance that endangers human health 
or the environment, or any non-compliance with Prohibitions A.1 through A.13 contained in the 
General Order, within 24 hours of becoming aware of its occurrence. None occurred during 2024.

provided by the Executive Officer (included as Exhibit 2), and shall include:

ANNUAL REPORTING NOTES AND LIMITATIONS 
 
Application volumes used in the 2024 Annual Report are reduced by 25 percent from volumes 
recorded in the irrigation records to account for the portion of water that runs off the fields and is 
recirculated into the process wastewater pond. Field 1 was not planted during the 2024 reporting 
year, and there were no land applications to Field 1. See the Notes section of the Annual Report 
(Exhibit 2, Page 43) for additional details regarding the data entered into the Annual Report.

GROUNDWATER REPORTING 
 
An individual network of groundwater monitoring wells was installed on the dairy in November 
2012, and sampling began in December 2013 in compliance with the approved site-specific well 
installation and monitoring plan. Results from the 2024 monitoring events are incorporated with this 
submittal as a separate report and included as Exhibit 6. 
 
STORMWATER REPORTING 
 
Tollcrest Dairy submitted a No Discharge Technical Report, Notice of Non-Applicability Order 
(NONA) 2014-0057-DWQ, to the RWQCB stormwater unit on June 12, 2015. The NONA was 
approved in a letter from the RWQCB dated July 15, 2015. Tollcrest Dairy is no longer required to 
maintain coverage under Order 2014-0057-DWQ (Stormwater General Order), and annual reports 
are no longer submitted. The NONA covers only the dairy facility and not the irrigated land 
associated with the dairy. 
 
No discharges from the property occurred in 2024.
