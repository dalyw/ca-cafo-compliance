Summary

Nine wells and one canal were sampled at the James Sousa facility on May 20, September 18, and
December 17,2024 by DeGroot Environmental personnel The samples collected are listed below.
Electrical conductivity (EC) was measured in the laboratory. Ammonium presence was measured in
the field using test strips by DeGroot Environmental personnel. Results were recorded on the
sampling record for each sample collected. Samples were collected in bottles provided by the testing
laboratory. The samples were placed in a cooler with ice packs and delivered to BSK Analytical
Laboratories, an ELAP laboratory. Well samples were analyzed as defined in the MRP, updated in
2013. The five year analysis was performed on approximately 20% of the wells sampled.
